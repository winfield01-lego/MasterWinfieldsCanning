Getting started

Add your pantry inventory under Canned Goods. The app automatically calculates age and flags anything canned 18 months ago or more.

Use Requests as a simple community request queue, and Empty Jars to keep track of jars, lids, and rings.

Data is stored locally in this browser. Use Export/Import to move your data to another device.
